cookies and other tracking tools

Cookies are small text files that the user's browser stores at the request of the websites it visits. Cookies can be used in different ways, and each use gives rise to a category of cookies that are technically identical but functionally different.

From a functional point of view, technical cookies, whose purpose is to make the site and its services work, are distinguished from profiling cookies, whose purpose is not related to the operation of the site but to analysing the profiles and preferences of its visitors; analytics cookies sit in between, and can be treated as technical or as profiling cookies depending on whether the data collected can be traced back to a specific person. Analytics cookies are therefore further divided into anonymous analytics cookies, which do not collect information identifying the user, and identifying ones, which make it possible to trace the data collected back to the user.

Anonymous analytics cookies neither collect nor contain identifying information; since, thanks to the technical anonymisation measures adopted, they cannot lead to the identification of the user, the law in force ( Guidelines on cookies and other tracking tools of the Italian Data Protection Authority, 10 June 2021 ) does not require the user's consent for their use. Since these cookies are managed directly by third parties, please refer to their respective notices for further details.

Identifying analytics cookies, on the other hand, may contain information that can lead to the identification of the user, and the law in force therefore requires the user's prior consent for their use. In this case too, since these cookies are managed directly by third parties, please refer to their respective notices for further details.

The use of profiling cookies and of other tools for tracking users' profiles, preferences and personal data in general always requires the data subject's prior consent.

As regards their origin, first-party cookies, set directly by the data controller named above, are distinguished from third-party cookies, set by external parties whose services are included in the site to add further features to it.

As regards how long they remain on the user's device, session cookies, which are deleted automatically at the end of the browsing session or when the browser is closed, are distinguished from persistent cookies, which remain stored until their expiry date or until they are deleted by the site or by the user.
